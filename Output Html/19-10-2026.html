UNIFIL in Lebanon

★ ★ ☆ ☆ ☆

Source: TH

The UN has stressed the need to sustain stability in southern Lebanon as the final mandate of the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) approaches its end.

  • About Lebanon: Lebanon is a country on the eastern coast of the Mediterranean Sea, bordered by Syria and Israel, with Beirut as its capital.
    • Historically, Lebanon was home to the Phoenicians, whose coastal cities, including Tyre, Sidon and Byblos, served as major trading centres during the Bronze Age.
      • Hezbollah remains a pivotal domestic actor within Lebanon's political landscape and a key combatant in regional friction with Israel.
      • Also, the Blue Line is a UN-demarcated withdrawal line between Lebanon and Israel, established in 2000 to confirm Israeli withdrawal from southern Lebanon.
  • About UNIFIL: The United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) was established by the UN Security Council in March 1978 to verify Israel’s withdrawal from Lebanon, restore peace and security, and help the Lebanese Government re-establish its authority in southern Lebanon.
    • Its mandate was revised in 1982 and 2000. Following the 2006 conflict, UNIFIL’s responsibilities were expanded to include monitoring the cessation of hostilities, supporting the deployment of the Lebanese Armed Forces, facilitating humanitarian assistance.
    • UNSC Resolution 2790 (2025) extended UNIFIL for the final time until 31 December 2026.
    • India has contributed to UNIFIL since 1998. As of September 2026, 600+ Indian peacekeepers served in the mission.

KAUMARACON-2026

★ ★ ☆ ☆ ☆

Source: PIB

The Vice-President inaugurated the 3rd International Conference on Kaumarabhritya, KAUMARACON-2026 , organised by the All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA).

  • The theme of the Conference was “Shaping the Future of Child Health: Global Perspectives, Research Innovations, and Clinical Frontiers in Kaumarabhritya.”
    • The conference stressed combining traditional knowledge with scientific research to improve evidence-based healthcare and global acceptance of Ayurveda.
    • The event saw the launch of the AYUSH Start-up Portal, AIIA-iCAINE website, Ayurveda Digital Innovation and Venture Acceleration Facility (AyuDIVA), Vamana App and Panchakarma Assessment App .
      • Panchakarma, meaning “five actions” in Sanskrit, is an Ayurvedic treatment system that aims to remove toxins (Ama), balance the three doshas, Vata, Pitta and Kapha , and improve overall health and well-being.
  • About Ayurveda: A yurveda is one of the oldest traditional systems of medicine .  Its Brihat Trayi comprises the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita and Ashtanga Hridayam.
    • The Ministry of AYUSH, established in 2014 , promotes traditional systems of medicine. The Ministry has undertaken several initiatives to promote traditional medicine such as:
      • Establishment of the WHO Global Traditional Medicine Centre in Jamnagar,
      • Integration of traditional medicine classifications into ICD-11
      • Creation of the Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL)
      • Implementation of the National AYUSH Mission (NAM)
  • About Kaumarabhritya: It is the branch of Ayurveda concerned with child healthcare , including nutrition, growth, development and disease prevention.
    • Kaumara: Derived from 'Kumara', pertaining to a child from birth up to 16 years of age .
    • Bhritya: Derived from the Sanskrit root 'Bhri'- meaning nourishment, upbringing, support, maintenance, and protection.
